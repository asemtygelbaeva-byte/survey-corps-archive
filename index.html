The three walls

Wall Maria, Wall Rose and Wall Sina protect the people of Paradis from Titans.

The outermost line

Wall Maria

The first boundary against the outside world. Its fall in year 845 changes Eren, Mikasa and Armin’s lives forever.

The second defence

Wall Rose

The middle wall shelters those displaced from Maria. Trost, its southern district, becomes a crucial test of humanity’s resolve.

The protected centre

Wall Sina

The innermost ring surrounds the royal capital. Here, comfortable lives sit alongside carefully protected secrets.

City stills are atmospheric illustrations; they are not a map of the three walls.
