Field questions

Is this an official Attack on Titan website?

No. It is a student-made fan archive. Official news and releases are available from shingeki.tv.

What happens when I check the form?

Your browser checks the required fields, email format and text length, then opens an end note on this page. Only the selected series-experience option appears in the page address. Your name, email and message are not submitted. This is a practice activity, so no application is sent or saved.

Can I explore without spoilers?

The home page stays close to the opening premise. The lore page labels later revelations; the Titan table, battle gallery and later season guides contain spoilers.
